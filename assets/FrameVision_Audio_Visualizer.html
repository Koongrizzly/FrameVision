FrameVision Audio Visualizer Help

Load and play music

Drop a local audio file anywhere, or use Browse Audio. Files remain local. Chrome and Edge support the widest range of formats. Space plays or pauses; arrows seek five seconds; F toggles fullscreen; H hides the interface; M mutes; R restarts.

Beat detection and BPM

The visualizer combines bass energy, spectral flux, adaptive histories, transient peaks, and cooldown protection. BPM is an estimate, not sample-perfect timing. If the pulse feels early or late, use Beat Offset. Select Manual BPM or tap Tap BPM several times in rhythm to lock the visual beat clock.

Automatic scenes

Auto mode uses a non-repeating shuffle bag of enabled scenes. Choose a beat, bar-like, timed, or intelligent interval. Major impacts can trigger an earlier transition, while quiet sections lower visual intensity.

Recording

Record Visualizer captures only the rendered canvas plus the playing track—never the microphone. Browser-native recording usually creates WebM, not MP4. Stop manually or let the track end, then save the recording.

Performance and safety

Auto quality gradually reduces pixel ratio, particles, and expensive detail when frame time rises. Lower resolution or particles first on slower systems. Strobe safety is enabled by default and limits flash rate, white flashes, and camera shake. Reduced Motion further softens movement.
